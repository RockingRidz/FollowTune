Tracking & Steering
Dual IR receiver setup mounted on the front bumper reads signal strength from the Ripstik beacon.

Calculates side-to-side signal differences in real time to steer the motors smoothly.

Adjusts wheel speeds on the fly so the car curves right along with your board instead of making sharp, sudden jerks.

Center-Lock Alignment
Includes an LDR light sensor on the car aligned with a red laser module on the Ripstik.

Confirms the car is positioned directly behind you whenever the laser hits the sensor.

Gives the motors a slight speed boost along straight paths once center alignment is locked in.

Obstacle Evasion
Front-mounted ultrasonic sensor actively checks for obstacles in the car's path.

Servo motor swivels the sensor left and right to scan available clearance when something gets too close.

Overrides normal tracking to back up and pivot around objects before resuming the chase.

Kinetic Motion Synth
Turns the robot into a rolling instrument whenever Bluetooth is disconnected.

Converts your physical carving sways into dynamic musical pitches through the speaker.

Laser hits trigger high-frequency tone accents to give feedback while you ride.

Bluetooth & Audio
Onboard amplifier board drives a 3W speaker mounted directly on the chassis.

Streams music or custom sound effects wirelessly from your phone during normal tracking.

Switches back and forth between media streaming and synth mode based on connection status.
